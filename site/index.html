Everything between your computer and your PS5

One app for the whole round trip, built on its own fast transfer protocol.

Fast uploads

Folders, game images and archives go straight to the console over an encrypted, resumable connection. Thousands of small files are packed together so they don't crawl.

Install packages

Stream a .pkg from your computer, a link or a NAS while it installs, or copy it first. Updates and DLC install from each game's own page.

Convert games

Turn a game folder into a package, an exFAT image or a compressed .ffpfsc for ShadowMount+, with AMPR LZ4 asset packs to make images smaller.

Your collection

Every game on your drives and network shares, grouped by game with its updates, DLC and duplicates, and what each console is missing.

Games, saves and files

Launch, stop and uninstall games, back up and restore saves, browse the console's drives, and copy straight from one PS5 to another.

Desktop, phone or browser

Native apps for macOS, Windows, Linux and Android, and a Docker image that serves the same app as a web page from your NAS. In 21 languages.
